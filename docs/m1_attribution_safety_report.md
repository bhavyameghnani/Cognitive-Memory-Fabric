## 1. The vulnerability (reproduced in the audit, now closed)
An advisor's statement could become an active **confirmed semantic memory**: `guard_certainty` used the speaker only for `recommendation`, `MemoryWriteValidator` checked that the candidate's role equalled the turn's speaker but not that a *confirmed* item may be advisor-spoken, `Consolidator.eligible` and `approve_semantic` never looked at the speaker, and the integrity boundary's "derived" verification required only that a verified episode of the same turn stated the same value. The semantic row then carried `source_role = System`. A sentence such as "Please don't treat it as final yet" also passed as `confirmed`. [audit Traces B, C-5, D, executed]

## 2. What was implemented
### 2.1 One deterministic policy (`src/agents/memory/attribution.py`, new)
* **Attribution classes**, set from the **cited turn's speaker in the data, never from the model**: `client_direct`, `advisor_reported_client` ("you've decided…", "your decision to…", "the working plan I'm recording…", "here's where we landed…"), `advisor_interpretation`, `system_inference`, `ambiguous` (third party or no stated speaker).
* **Ceilings.** Only client-attributable evidence supports `confirmed` / `correction`. An advisor statement is client-confirmed only when a **client-spoken evidence row** affirms it (a short, unhedged affirmation) and is cited. Everything else is capped at `unconfirmed`. `recommendation`, `exploratory`, `hypothetical` and `unconfirmed` are weaker than the ceiling and are never changed. **Downgrade only**: a test checks that no input label is ever raised.
* **Client confirmation discovery** (`find_client_confirmation`): in the real data the client's reply follows almost every advisor recap ("That's right." `INT-061#12`, "Correct." `INT-001#14`, "That sounds right to me." `INT-060#14`). The *very next* turn, spoken by the Client, a short affirmation with no hedge/negation, is cited as a supporting evidence row. It never looks further than the next turn. Without this the policy would remove nearly all legitimate semantic memory.
* **Finality / negation, in context** (`finality`): explicit non-final statements ("don't treat it as final yet", "I haven't decided", "nothing has been decided", "this is only a possibility", "I'm still considering it", "I may change my mind", "this is not confirmed", "I'll confirm on Wednesday") cap a would-be confirmed claim at `unconfirmed` (or `exploratory` for open-ended wording). A negation in the **cited** sentence decides; in **another** sentence of the same turn it decides only when anaphoric (it/this/that/the decision) or generic; deferral and "subject to/tentative" count only in the cited sentence (they are usually logistics elsewhere: "I'll confirm when it arrives"); past-perfect negations elsewhere are history, not current status ("in March you hadn't decided"). A negation together with a positive decision in one sentence, or an unresolvable scope, is **unresolved**: capped at `unconfirmed` and flagged `needs review` (abstain, never guess).

### 2.2 Where the same policy is enforced (no single point of failure)
| Path | Change | File |
|---|---|---|
| Rule extraction | `enforce_policy` on every kept decision | `ingest.py` (`filter_memory_candidates`) |
| LLM-first extraction | `enforce_policy` after the verifier vote, value check and slot check: a confident extractor/verifier cannot exceed the ceiling | `llm_first.py` (`run`) |
| Persistence | `persist_decision` re-applies the policy to exactly what is stored and re-derives status/event type when it lowers | `ingest.py` |
| Harness write validator | same policy recomputed from the data; `approve_semantic` refuses a creator that is not client-attributable | `harness/memory_write_validator.py` |
| Consolidation | `eligible(ep, client_ok)` / `fold(..., attributable)`: advisor-spoken (or recorded advisor-reported) episodes never create or reinforce a semantic version unless a client-spoken row confirms them (also legacy rows); a correction supersedes an earlier decision only if it is itself client-attributable | `consolidate.py` |
| **Integrity boundary** (every durable write, including direct callers) | `_attribution_gate`: (1) the stored `source_role` must be the cited turn's real speaker (a semantic memory can no longer be `System`-sourced or have no source); (2) a `confirmed`/`correction` memory needs client-attributable, final evidence, else `MemoryWriteRejected("attribution_ceiling")` / `("source_attribution_mismatch")`. Human corrections (`human_correction`) are a separate authorised path and are not changed. | `integrity.py` |
| Semantic provenance | the semantic row's `source_role` = the creating evidence's speaker; its `basis` records `client_direct` or `advisor_reported_client, client-confirmed`; an advisor-sourced version also copies the **client's confirming row** into its evidence | `consolidate.py` |

### 2.3 How each requirement is met
* **Advisor-reported decision, no client confirmation:** kept as an episodic memory, `certainty = unconfirmed`, `status = unconfirmed`, `source_role = Advisor`, `event_type = reported_decision`, evidence = the advisor's exact sentence. Nothing is discarded; it creates no semantic memory and supersedes nothing. [RUN: tests 3, 8, audit trace D]
* **With client confirmation:** `confirmed`, the memory cites both rows (advisor primary, client supporting) and remains `source_role = Advisor`; the semantic version is `Advisor`-sourced with the client row in its evidence. [RUN: test 5b]
* **Provenance preserved:** semantic memories keep speaker, evidence rows, attribution class and certainty; `System` never replaces a source role. [RUN: tests 1, 5, 5b]
* **A later advisor recap cannot overwrite a direct client statement:** advisor-only 60 after the client's 63 leaves 63 active and stores 60 as `unconfirmed`. [RUN: test 8]
* **Idempotency and history:** the 65→63 chain keeps v1 `superseded` with `valid_to`, `validate_chains` clean, re-ingest skipped, forced re-ingest adds 0 rows. [RUN: test 1, both modes]

## 7. Limitations and risks (read before relying on this)
1. **Recall.** The policy is deliberately conservative. Advisor statements with no client-spoken reply in the very next turn now stay `unconfirmed`, and sibling-sentence negations that cannot be scoped abstain. Whether this costs real-model recall has **not** been measured (no API key); Bhavya's measured recall (84–90% on synthetic data) must be re-measured after M1.
2. **Wording rules are mechanism, not meaning.** The negation, reporting and affirmation patterns are English regular expressions tuned on the phrases named in the brief and on this dataset's turns; their precision/recall on real conversations is **unmeasured**. They can miss paraphrases ("let's not call it settled") and can over-fire (a negation I could not scope).
3. **The client's earlier statement is not merged with the advisor's later recap.** When a client states a decision (e.g. `INT-125#4`) and the advisor re-states it with no reply, the recap is now an `unconfirmed` open item rather than corroboration. Merging it into the client's memory is future work.
4. **Confirmation is the next turn only** and uses a short list of affirmations; "Yes, absolutely, go ahead and file it." beyond 20 words, or a confirmation two turns later, is not recognised.
5. **Legacy data is not migrated.** The policy applies to **new** writes. The shipped `data/seed/cmf_memory_llm_first.db` (read-only query, not modified) holds 89 of 181 `confirmed`/`correction` episodes spoken by the advisor (26 have a client-spoken evidence row), and **20 of its 55 semantic versions rest on advisor-spoken primary evidence with no client-spoken row**; all 55 are labelled `System`. The consolidator now excludes such rows from future versions, but existing rows remain until a re-verification step (a later milestone) is run on a copy.
6. **`confidence` is untouched** except that a lowered claim's bucket confidence is capped at 0.3; the numeric value remains a calibration bucket, not a probability of truth.
7. **Supporting-evidence additions** (`boundary.support`) are not attribution-gated: they add evidence rows and counts, never certainty or values, and consolidation only adds supports from eligible episodes.
8. **Human corrections and procedures are unchanged.** `human_correction` is a separate authorised path; the curated and proposed-procedure flows are not affected.
9. **Not a semantic-accuracy claim.** Everything here is deterministic gating plus scripted-model mechanism tests; no real LLM was run and no human validated any label.
